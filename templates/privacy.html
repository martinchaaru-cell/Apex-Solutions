Privacy notice

How Apex Solutions handles the personal data you share through this website, under the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

Apex Solutions ("we") is a technology company based in Kenya. For enquiries sent through this website, we are the data controller.{% if contact.email %} You can contact us about your data at {{ contact.email }}.{% endif %}

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect your name, email address, and, if you choose to give them, your phone number and organisation name, together with the service you are interested in, your budget range and your message.

Our server also records basic technical information needed to run and protect the site, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These logs do not contain your enquiry.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal if you ask for one.
  • To protect the website against spam and abuse.

We rely on your consent, given when you send the form, and on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not sell or share them with anyone for their own use.

Cookies

This website sets one strictly necessary cookie that protects the enquiry form against forged submissions. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Where it is stored and for how long

Enquiries are stored on a server we manage, rented from a hosting provider, with encrypted backups. Access is limited to the people who handle enquiries. We keep enquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them, unless they become part of a signed contract.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. To do any of these, contact us using the details on our contact page. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, security headers and a hardened server, and is built to Level 1 of the Apex Security Baseline. No system is completely secure, but if a breach affected your data we would act promptly and notify you and the regulator as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top of this page.
