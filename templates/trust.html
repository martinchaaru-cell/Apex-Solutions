Why trust Apex?

Three levels of protection, chosen at discovery and written into your agreement.

Level 1

Content

Websites with no logins. Secure headers, TLS, protected forms, hardened server, tested backups.

Level 2

Application

Systems with logins. Everything in Level 1, plus hashed passwords, permission tests for every page and record, and a second independent code review.

Level 3

High risk

POS, payments, school and finance systems. Everything in Level 2, plus manual review of all money and permission code, and an external penetration test through a certified partner.
